## Background

- Early visual input at birth is low-acuity and rod-dominated.
- This project tests what useful visual information may still remain available.
- Focus: whether face-related signals are still computationally decodable under newborn-like degradation.

## Research Questions

- How much visual structure remains under rod-weighted, low-acuity conditions?
- Is this reduced visual information sufficient for face-relevant discrimination tasks?
- Which signals remain robust, and which collapse under degradation?
